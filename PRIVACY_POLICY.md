# Pro To-Do List Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-19

Pro To-Do List stores tasks, priorities, categories, due dates, XP, and streak information locally on the device using local app storage.

The app does not currently create user accounts, upload task data to a server, or sell personal information. Scheduled reminders are generated on the device and are used only for task notifications.

You can remove locally stored data from the Settings screen using Clear all data. Uninstalling the app may also remove its local data, depending on the device settings.

The app may request notification permission so task reminders can be shown. Notifications can be disabled from Android system settings.

This policy may be updated when cloud sync, analytics, or other online services are added. Contact: supportskywins@gmail.com
